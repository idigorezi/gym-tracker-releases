Built for the front desk

Everything you need. Nothing you'll have to learn.

No spreadsheets, no paper ledgers, no guessing who's behind. Open the app in the morning and the list is already made.

WhatsApp reminders in one click

Gym Tracker writes the right message for each member, whether it's before, on or after their due date, and opens WhatsApp with it ready to go. Send to one person or work through everyone who's late.

To: Abebe Kebede

Who has to pay, today

One screen shows who's overdue, who's due soon and how much is left to collect, sorted so the most urgent come first.

Members & plans

Register a member in seconds, put them on a monthly plan and see their full payment history on their own page.

Record payments fast

Take a payment and their paid-until date moves on by itself. No date math, no mistakes at the counter.

Monthly reports

See income per month and every payment taken in a month, so you always know how the gym is doing.
